HOW TO MAKE THE 3-HOUR KALEIDOSCOPE VIDEO
==========================================

What you get: a 3-hour 1080p video of the photoreal neon kaleidoscope you
approved (real glass gems, gold, stone, strong glow, drifting camera,
glowing mist). Only a 10-minute seamless loop is rendered; it is played 18
times to make 3 hours, with no visible join.


ONE-TIME SETUP
--------------

1. Unzip PortalMachine.zip somewhere with plenty of free space, for example
   your Documents folder. If you have an older PortalMachine folder, you can
   delete it first (keep anything you want from its "output" folder).

2. Double-click setup.bat in the PortalMachine folder (quick if Python and
   the other tools are already installed from before).

3. Install Blender 4.5 LTS (free) from https://www.blender.org/download/lts/
   Use the normal Windows installer (.msi) and its default install location,
   so PortalMachine can find it automatically.
   It must be 4.5, not Blender 5: this scene renders extremely slowly in
   Blender 5. If you already have Blender 5, keep it and install 4.5 next to
   it; PortalMachine picks 4.5 automatically.

4. Update your NVIDIA graphics driver (GeForce Experience / NVIDIA App, or
   https://www.nvidia.com/drivers). Blender needs a recent driver to use your
   RTX 3060's ray-tracing cores (OptiX).


BEFORE A LONG RENDER: SPEED TEST (a few minutes)
------------------------------------------------

5. Open the folder  PortalMachine\weekly\2026-09-26_kaleidoscope
   and double-click  speed_test.bat

   - The first line must say "on the graphics card (OPTIX)". If it says
     "on the CPU", Blender did not find the graphics card: update the driver
     (step 4) and try again.
   - The "10-minute" line is how long the whole 3-hour video will take.


PREPARE THE LAPTOP
------------------

6. Plug in the charger. Rendering at full power for days on battery is not
   possible.

7. Stop Windows from sleeping: Settings > System > Power & battery >
   Screen and sleep > set "When plugged in, put my device to sleep after"
   to Never. (The screen may turn off; that is fine.)

8. Keep the laptop on a hard surface with the vents clear; it will run hot
   for a long time. A laptop cooling pad helps.

9. Free disk space needed: about 15 GB (the finished video is up to about
   13.5 GB, plus working space).


RENDER THE VIDEO
----------------

10. In the same folder, double-click  render.bat  and answer:

      Video length ....................... press Enter  (3 hours)
      Resolution ......................... press Enter  (1080p fast)
      Seamless loop length ............... press Enter  (10 minutes)
      Music file ......................... drag your music file into the
                                           window and press Enter, or just
                                           press Enter for no sound

    The music repeats back to back for the full 3 hours, exactly as the
    track plays (no gaps added between repeats), and fades out over the
    last 5 seconds of the video.

11. Leave it running. The window shows progress and a time estimate (ETA).
    You can keep using the computer lightly, but it will be slow.

12. When it finishes, the output folder opens by itself. Your video is:

      PortalMachine\output\kaleidoscope_1.mp4


IF IT GETS INTERRUPTED (power cut, crash, restart, window closed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing finished is lost; work is saved every minute of video.
Just double-click render.bat again. It asks:

      Finish this interrupted render? [Y/n]

Press Enter (or Y) and it continues where it stopped.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- "1080p fast" renders at 1600x900 and upscales: about 30% faster and only a
  touch softer, which suits the lens look. "1080p sharp" renders every pixel.
- The 10-second samples you approved are part of this exact video (same
  seed), so the full render looks like what you saw, but sharper (1080p,
  more samples).
